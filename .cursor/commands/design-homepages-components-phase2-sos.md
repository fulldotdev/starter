# Marketing homepages - Components phase 2 (SOS polish)

Apply a major SOS polish pass to `src/pages/1.astro` through `src/pages/5.astro`.

## Goal

Take the phase-1 component drafts from "clean and simple" to high-end, expressive, and memorable while keeping core page structure and content intact.

## Rules

- Keep components-first architecture from phase 1; do not revert to raw HTML-heavy pages.
- Preserve section flow and information architecture, but significantly upgrade visual execution.
- Push art direction hard: typography, contrast, hierarchy, spacing rhythm, depth, and composition.
- Add rich but intentional polish: advanced states, micro-interactions, tasteful animation, layered backgrounds/textures, and stronger visual motifs.
- Use Tailwind/token utilities as baseline; add custom CSS/JS when needed for high-impact effects.
- Keep changes production-minded: responsive, performant, and accessible.

## Quality bar (required)

- Each of the 5 pages must feel premium and clearly more refined than phase 1.
- Each page needs a distinct visual personality, not just color swaps.
- Avoid generic templates and low-effort styling.

Use your frontend design skill aggressively in this phase.
