# Marketing homepages - Sauce layer

Take `src/pages/1.astro` through `src/pages/5.astro` and apply a major visual polish pass.

## Goal

Push the designs to an exceptional, unique, premium level. Keep the core structure/content, but dramatically upgrade visual direction and execution.

## Rules

- Keep existing component/page structure as the base.
- Upgrade typography, hierarchy, contrast, composition rhythm, and visual storytelling.
- Add bold but intentional motifs per page (not generic template aesthetics).
- Use Tailwind as baseline for layout/system consistency.
- You may use vanilla CSS extensively when it clearly improves quality (advanced effects, layered backgrounds, custom motion, masks, textures, expressive micro-interactions).
- You may use lightweight JS for progressive enhancement when it adds meaningful polish.
- Keep pages responsive, performant, and accessible.

## Quality bar (required)

- Each page must feel clearly more refined than before.
- Each of the 5 pages must have a distinct personality.
- Avoid low-effort color swaps; deliver real art direction upgrades.

Use your frontend design skill aggressively and push the limits of your design capabilities.
