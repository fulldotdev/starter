## Mastering the Content Layer

The content layer is a powerful feature of Fulldev UI:

1. **File Structure**: Organize your content in `src/content/pages/`.
2. **Markdown Power**: Use markdown for easy content creation and formatting.
3. **Front Matter**: Leverage front matter for metadata and component selection.

For more detailed information, refer to the [Fulldev UI docs](https://ui.full.dev/overview/content-layer) on the content layer.
